# About page stage 4 audit

## What to keep

- The verified career story, education, availability, portrait caption and biryani principle.
- The existing blue and warm-accent colour direction, serif editorial accent, light/dark themes and restrained reveal motion.
- The four useful "What I Bring" themes and the existing contact/work routes.

## What to compress

- Limit the hero to the two strongest paragraphs so its message and portrait read as one introduction.
- Reduce the quote's type scale, width and vertical space.
- Combine education and availability into one compact information panel.

## What to move

- Move the two broader career paragraphs from the hero into a dedicated Professional Evolution section.
- Place availability with the hero on desktop and after the portrait on mobile.
- Give education a purposeful position immediately before the closing action.

## What to redesign

- Replace the detached hero composition with a 7/5 editorial split and an integrated patterned portrait frame.
- Replace the heavy full-width evolution band with a divided, two-column editorial section.
- Turn "What I Bring" into an alternating 7/5 then 5/7 bento with semantic labels and distinct low-opacity patterns.
- End the page with a clear, restrained CTA containing both context and actions.

## Desktop and mobile concerns

- Desktop should reveal the start of the next section on a typical laptop and keep copy lines readable.
- The bento should preserve equal visual weight without forcing equal text lengths.
- Mobile must place portrait before availability, use one-column cards in narrative order, avoid oversized quote type and remain free of horizontal overflow at 320px.
- Motion must reveal once and become static under reduced-motion preferences.

## Final hierarchy

1. About hero
2. Professional evolution
3. What I Bring
4. Working principle
5. Education and availability
6. Closing CTA
